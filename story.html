How It All Began

Our Story

01

2019

How We Met

It was a rainy Tuesday afternoon at a friend's birthday gathering. Galilee was leaving early, and Karl arrived late (as always). Their eyes met by the coat rack — and the rest is history. They talked for hours, completely forgetting everyone else in the room.

02

2020

Our First Date

A quiet dinner at a little Italian restaurant. Karl had rehearsed his jokes, and Galilee had changed outfits multiple times. The food was forgettable, but the laughter — unforgettable. They stayed until closing time, then walked under the city lights.

03

December 2024

The Proposal

Karl planned for months — Batangas beach, fairy lights, Galilee's favorite flowers. He got down on one knee as the sun touched the horizon. Galilee said yes instantly. Both cried. Their families were hiding behind the palm trees, cheering them on.

04

May 14, 2026

The Wedding

And now — the beginning of forever. After years of inside jokes, late-night drives, and growing together, Karl & Galilee are ready to make it official. They want to share this moment with every person who has been part of their journey. Thank you for being here.
