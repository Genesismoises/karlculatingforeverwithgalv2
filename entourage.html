Dressing the Day

We'd love for our guests to complement the warmth and elegance of our celebration. Below is a guide to help you dress beautifully for our special day.

Principal Sponsors

Our honored witnesses, dressed to stand with us

🤵

Gentlemen

Coffee Brown · Beige · Cream
Barong Tagalog

A traditional and elegant choice that honors the spirit of a Filipino celebration.

👗

Ladies

Coffee Brown · Beige · Cream
Long Dress

Flowing gowns in warm earth tones that echo the grace of the ceremony.

✦
Guests

Celebrate with us in soft, joyful hues

🤵

Gentlemen

Blush · Sage · Nude Tones
Semi-Formal

Slacks, polo, or barong in gentle, muted tones. Keep it polished and comfortable.

👒

Ladies

Blush · Sage · Nude Tones
Semi-Formal

Midi dresses, skirts, or smart ensembles in soft, romantic shades.

🚫

Please avoid wearing white, ivory, or red — these are reserved for the bridal party and ceremony.
